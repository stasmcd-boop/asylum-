Before you contact us

I am already in the US. Can you help prepare my case materials?

Yes. We help organise your genuine history, timeline and evidence for a qualified lawyer. Legal strategy, filing procedures and representation are matters for a licensed professional. We agree the scope and price of our work before payment.

What if I have not chosen a country?

Just tell us. We start with your goals, documents, timeline and budget, then discuss destinations and questions to check.

Do you only help with asylum in the US?

No. We work with the US, European countries and other destinations on request. We help with destination selection, routes, document preparation and practical support. Asylum preparation is one of our services.

Can LGBTQ+ people and same-sex couples contact you?

Yes. We support LGBTQ+ people, couples and families. You do not need to disclose your sexual orientation, gender identity or personal documents in your first enquiry.

What is free and what is paid?

The initial review lasts 30 minutes and is free: we clarify your task, discuss options and the next step. We agree any paid work, its scope and price before payment. Tickets, accommodation and other external expenses are separate.

Request a free initial review

We usually reply within a couple of hours. The free initial review lasts 30 minutes. One contact is enough; you do not need to choose a country.
